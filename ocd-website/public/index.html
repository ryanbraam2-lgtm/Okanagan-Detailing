Our Story

Born in the Okanagan

Okanagan Car Detail was founded on a simple belief: every vehicle deserves to be treated like a show car. Based in Lake Country, BC — nestled between the mountains and the lake — we bring the same obsessive precision to every detail appointment that the Okanagan brings to everything it produces.

Whether you're protecting a weekend sports car, refreshing a daily driver, or preserving a collector's piece, our team approaches every vehicle with the same relentless attention to quality. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just flawless results.
